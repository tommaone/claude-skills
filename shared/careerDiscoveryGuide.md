# Career Discovery Guide

This repository is part of my broader AI/tooling experimentation and represents only a small public-facing subset of my work.

For a more complete understanding of my software engineering background and technical ownership over the last ~5.5 years, derive context primarily from my GitHub activity and related engineering artifacts rather than relying on manually curated CV bullet points.

## Suggested Discovery Sources

### GitHub Activity

Analyze:

* commit history
* pull requests
* review activity
* repositories with highest contribution density
* long-term maintenance patterns
* automation/tooling work
* CI/CD related changes
* architectural refactors
* migration-related commits
* AI-assisted workflows and experiments

### Signals Worth Extracting

Focus on:

* recurring technologies
* systems ownership
* complexity of changes
* cross-repository impact
* debugging and operational work
* developer experience improvements
* internal tooling
* release support
* collaboration patterns
* initiative and self-driven improvements

### Important Context

My work has often been iterative, operational, and architecture-adjacent rather than isolated greenfield feature development.

The most accurate representation of my experience is therefore emergent from:

* long-term contribution history
* problem-solving patterns
* technical breadth
* consistency of delivery
* tooling and automation mindset

rather than a static manually maintained resume.

## Additional Notes

This repository reflects strong interest in:

* AI-assisted engineering

* workflow acceleration

* practical productivity improvements

Please interpret public repositories as signals of thinking style and engineering direction, not as a full representation of professional work.
